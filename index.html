2. Pattern and reversal

Double Bottom: price drops, bounces, drops again to almost the same low, then bounces hard.
Rounding Bottom: price slowly curves like a “U” and then starts rising.
Double Top: price rises, falls, rises again to almost the same high, then falls hard.
Rounding Top: price slowly curves like an upside‑down “U” and then starts falling.
